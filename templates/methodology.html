METHODOLOGY

Two-stage research flow

01

Preprocessing module

Radiographs are validated, converted to RGB, resized to 224 x 224, and normalized into the format required for MobileNet-compatible inference.

02

GA-optimized MobileNet

A genetic algorithm tunes the classification threshold to improve discrimination between normal and TB-suspect lung patterns.

03

Clinical interpretation layer

Scores and confidence values are presented with a clear disclaimer to reinforce research-only usage and physician review.
